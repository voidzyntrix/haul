Download

  • Android Android 10 or newer, 64-bit. Free with 5 downloads a day, or one payment for more. Get it on Google Play
  • Windows Windows 10 and 11. One program, no installer. Free and unlimited. Haul-Windows.exe
  • macOS The app in a zip. The first time, right-click it and choose Open. Free and unlimited. Haul-macOS.zip
  • Linux Extract it and run ./Haul. Free and unlimited. Haul-Linux.tar.gz

iPhone and iPad: iOS doesn't allow an app like this, so there is no Haul for them. The computer apps aren't signed by a publisher yet, so Windows and macOS ask you once to confirm you want to open them.

Nothing leaves your device

  • No server in between. The engine (yt-dlp and ffmpeg) runs inside the app. Your links go straight from your device to the site that hosts the video, and nowhere else.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads. Haul keeps no history of what you download. Your settings stay on the device.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. Haul never sees your payment details; Google Play only tells the app which plan your account owns.
  • This page is just as quiet. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from anywhere else, and its security policy stops it from connecting to anything.

Save only what you have the right to keep, and respect each site's rules and the people who made the video. Haul does not save DRM-protected streams.

Haul uses yt-dlp and ffmpeg, which are separate projects under their own licences.
